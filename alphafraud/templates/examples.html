Examples — AlphaFold's blind spots, up close

Eight of AlphaFold's worst calls on freshly-deposited human structures, split into two kinds of failure. The first four are ordinary single-chain globular proteins — a flexible phosphatase scaffold, a Ca²⁺-sensing switch, a two-domain co-chaperone and a tiny WW module — where AlphaFold gets the fold right but commits, often at very high confidence, to the wrong conformation or domain arrangement. The last four are the dramatic whole-fold catastrophes: a novel membrane amyloid, a disordered monomer, a folded tetramer that amyloids, and a multidomain zymogen caught in the wrong biological context.
